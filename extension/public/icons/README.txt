Extension icon assets.

Source files (checked in):
  icon.svg        — 48 / 128 px master. Three diff lines + speech bubble.
  icon-small.svg  — 16 / 32 px master. Two thicker diff lines, larger bubble.

Built PNGs (regenerated by the script below):
  icon-16.png   from icon-small.svg
  icon-32.png   from icon-small.svg
  icon-48.png   from icon.svg
  icon-128.png  from icon.svg

To regenerate on macOS:

  cd extension/public/icons
  qlmanage -t -s 512 -o . icon.svg       # → icon.svg.png at 512x512
  qlmanage -t -s 512 -o . icon-small.svg # → icon-small.svg.png at 512x512
  sips -z 16  16  icon-small.svg.png --out icon-16.png
  sips -z 32  32  icon-small.svg.png --out icon-32.png
  sips -z 48  48  icon.svg.png       --out icon-48.png
  sips -z 128 128 icon.svg.png       --out icon-128.png
  rm icon.svg.png icon-small.svg.png

On Linux: replace the qlmanage+sips pipeline with
  rsvg-convert -w 16  icon-small.svg > icon-16.png
  rsvg-convert -w 32  icon-small.svg > icon-32.png
  rsvg-convert -w 48  icon.svg       > icon-48.png
  rsvg-convert -w 128 icon.svg       > icon-128.png
